System 05

The Foundation

What holds underneath: identity, secrets, audit, and the ability to run with the cable out.

Read the chapter 6 parts in this system

Privacy at the Boundary

Nothing departs the machine unscrubbed. A redaction layer strips personal data from every cloud-bound prompt and retains an audit-grade hash of what it removed.

How the gates run

The Lock Reaches the Index

Protecting an attachment purges its searchable shadow, after a confirm that states the cost in chunks; a locked file refuses indexing and previews alike. What a password hides, retrieval cannot quote.

Audit by Construction

The records that matter are append-only because the database refuses otherwise. Sync runs freeze on completion and change rows cannot be edited or deleted, so the history is evidence rather than a convention everyone agreed to follow.

See the audit trail

Secrets Outside the Repository

Database passwords are generated per machine into an owner-only file that is never committed, never passed on a command line, and rotated by deleting it. Three accounts, each holding only the rights its job needs.

Active Directory Sync

Users and security groups read from Active Directory over LDAPS, with the certificate verified, and mapped to DeskOps roles and teams. Read-only in both directions, off by default, and every run previewed before it is applied.

Sovereign & Portable

A desktop application whose entire stack - web server, database, AI engine - occupies one folder. It runs offline, installs nothing on the host, and travels on a USB stick.

The philosophy

A helpdesk that compounds

DeskOps rests on five convictions. Every feature on this page traces its lineage to at least one of them.

01
Sovereign
Your data serves you, on your machine. The stack, the database and the AI reside in a folder you own, and the system is complete without a single cloud account.
02
Trust is earned
The AI is never extended the benefit of the doubt. It cites its evidence, its permissions are determined by deterministic rules rather than its own judgement, and its inputs are scrubbed before they travel.
03
Right-brain
Walls, canvases, decks and dashboards. Where another system would produce a table, DeskOps endeavours to produce a picture, because people reason in pictures.
04
Self-learning
The system is built to study its own history - what was attempted, what actually worked - so the assistant sharpens against real outcomes rather than remaining frozen at installation.
05
Work compounds
A resolved ticket is not an ending. It feeds tag profiles, knowledge articles and playbooks, so the next comparable ticket arrives already half resolved.
06
Deterministic first
Wherever exactness matters - arithmetic, access decisions, schedules - rules and tools outrank the model. The generative parts of DeskOps operate strictly inside boundaries the deterministic parts define.
07
Reuse over reinvention
One shell, one component library, one dialog voice. A new screen assembles what already exists rather than inventing a variant, which is why 137 screens still read as one application.
08
One thing at a time
Features ship singly and are verified singly, never as sweeping rewrites. The 1,400-commit history is a ledger of small, provable steps, and the system's stability is the dividend.

Chapter 02

The Money

A helpdesk that keeps its own books. Genuine double-entry accounting underpins the module: every invoice and expense posts journal entries automatically, and a single billing pipeline governs every document type so the rules can never drift apart.

  • Chart of Accounts and General Ledger - orthodox double-entry, not a totals column posing as accounting
  • Quotes post nothing until accepted - a promise is not a transaction, and the ledger remains honest
  • Retainers as client credit - money received in advance stands as a balance and diminishes as work is billed
  • Budgets against actuals - targets per account category, tracked live, drawn as gauges by the application's own charts
  • Period close, reconciliation and audit - closing a month locks every ledger write dated inside it, and every posting leaves a trail

The Time

Hours are recorded where the work actually occurs, inside tickets and tasks, a few clicks deep, and are then consolidated into per-person and per-team views. The direction of travel is deliberately visual: walls of hours legible at a glance, rather than spreadsheet grids that demand decoding.

  • Logging in the flow - from the ticket, from the task list, without breaking stride
  • Roll-ups that answer questions - who is loaded, who is available, where the week actually went
  • Team-level utilisation - a distinct team surface, because an individual's calendar and a team's capacity are different questions

Chapter 05

Qleo, the AI Crew

The assistant has a name, Qleo (say CLEE-oh), and a residence: a local inference engine on the same machine as the data. Qleo triages, tags, drafts and reasons, and behind it operates a crew of deep agents that undertake whole investigations, planning their steps on a board and recording every action in a replayable ledger.

  • Local-first by architecture - the everyday brain runs on your hardware; five cloud models stand behind it as a fallback chain
  • An Access Agent immune to persuasion - requests are granted, escalated or queued by deterministic rules applied to the shape of the request, never its wording
  • A distillation pipeline - constructed to teach the small local model from a larger teacher, so the machine you own grows permanently more capable
  • The Studio - a coding agent that plans before it edits, modifying DeskOps from within DeskOps, every change on an undo ledger
  • No mental arithmetic - anything exact is delegated to deterministic tools Qleo must call; the model never computes in its head

Chapter 06

The Automation

Repetition is treated as a defect. Playbooks permit the person who knows the process to draw the automation, a trigger, conditions and actions wired together on a node canvas, and the application's own engine executes it when genuine events fire. No scripting, no third-party automation service, no data leaving the premises.

  • Drawn, then run - the canvas reads like a circuit diagram, and what is drawn is precisely what executes
  • Native scheduling - recurring jobs reside in the database engine itself, so automation survives restarts and requires no external scheduler
  • The compounding loop - the roadmap directs playbooks toward Qleo, so the system may propose automations from the patterns it observes in resolved work

    Research · Episode 1

    AI Causal Reasoning
    via RAG

    The first research ticket raised through DeskOps investigated one of the most contested questions in AI: does it reason causally, or is it a sophisticated pattern-matcher?

    Four academic papers were indexed as attachments. The RAG pipeline retrieved relevant chunks, a pre-screening pass filtered them for relevance, and the system generated a structured resolution note with inline citations grounded in the evidence.

    Q1 To what extent do current AI agents rely on correlation rather than causal reasoning?
    Q2 How can we detect when an agent is using spurious correlations instead of meaningful relationships?
    Q3 Are there reliable indicators that distinguish causal reasoning from pattern matching in model outputs?
    Q4 How does shortcut learning manifest in multi-step agent workflows?
    Cynefin Classification
    The resolution framework uses domain classification to calibrate tone - a Clear domain note is direct and factual, while a Complex domain note acknowledges uncertainty and avoids false certainty. The AI reasons through this silently before writing a single word.
    Bloom's Taxonomy Depth
    Each note is calibrated to the appropriate cognitive level - from recalling a known fix through to synthesising a novel approach from first principles. The depth of the output reflects the depth of the problem.
    Evidence-Grounded Citations
    Pass 1 screens every RAG chunk for genuine relevance before it reaches the generation model. Only excerpts that directly support a specific, named factual claim about the ticket topic survive. The result is a note where every citation earns its place.
    How the AI earns it

    Kept honest by design

    Every AI feature in DeskOps passes through the same four disciplines. None depends on the model behaving well; each is enforced around it.

    1
    Grounded
    Answers derive from the ticket's own indexed evidence - its files, description and history - never from the model's imagination. No evidence, no claim.
    2
    Local first
    A model running on the same machine as the data performs the everyday work. Cloud models are a contingency for the hardest tasks, never the default route.
    3
    Scrubbed
    Anything cloud-bound passes a redaction gate first. Names, emails and identifiers are stripped, and each removal is hashed so an audit trail exists without a copy of the secret.
    4
    Cited or silent
    Claims carry inline citations to their sources, and a screening pass removes any citation that cannot earn its place. Exact work - arithmetic, dates - is delegated to deterministic tools, never left to the model's mental math.
